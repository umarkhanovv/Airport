Under order no. 64-НҚ of 8 October 2024 from the Department of the Committee for
Regulation of Natural Monopolies of the Ministry of National Economy of the
Republic of Kazakhstan for Turkistan Region, Turkistan International Airport JSC
gives notice of the approved tariff for producing, transmitting, distributing and
supplying thermal energy. The tariff is 8,970.65 tenge per Gcal excluding VAT and
applies from 1 November 2024.

Tariffs in force for 2026 — published 29.10.2025.

Notice of regulated services for 2026 — published 26.12.2025.
