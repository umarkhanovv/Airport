TURKISTAN INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT IS A 100% INVESTMENT PROJECT OF “YDA INSAAT SANAYI VE TICARET ANONIM SIRKETI” JSC, WHICH OPERATES UNDER A PPP PROJECT (PUBLIC-PRIVATE PARTNERSHIP).Turkistan airport is designed in accordance with International Standards. To ensure the comfort and safety of passengers in accordance with European and IATA standards, the terminal is equipped with all the necessary safety and security requirements. The throughput is 450pass/hour. There are 2 telescopic ramps, 14 passenger check-in counters, Parking for 88 seats, as well as VIP and CIP halls.

Availability of space:

- Duty free
- Taxi
- Cafe-bars
- Luggage packing
- Beauty salon
- Various stores, such as gift shops, market, accessories, bookstore
- Ticket sales and travel agencies
- Pharmacies
- Offices for airlines and representative companies

Availability of space in the passenger terminal

- Integration of the baggage system and inspection equipment
- Automatic notification of passengers
- Automatic aircraft parking system for Parking
- Automated control system of passengers
- TPC system adapted for storing jet a-1 aviation fuel and lubricants

Turkistan airport is equipped with 2 telescopic passenger ladders, which have an additional function of an automatic Parking system.

Turkistan airport prepares and implements the entire range of works and services related to providing passenger, baggage, mail, cargo transportation by air, providing aircraft with air cargo and additional services. “Turkistan International Airport” LLP will provide airport ground handling services for aircraft, airlines and aircraft operators, regardless of their ownership forms, when performing regular and charter flights.
