If your baggage is not on the belt when you arrive, go to the Lost & Found desk
straight away.

If you have lost personal belongings inside Turkistan International Airport or
on board the aircraft, contact Lost & Found: everything handed in is kept there
for a set period.

If you find your baggage damaged when you collect it, go to Lost & Found to file
a report.
