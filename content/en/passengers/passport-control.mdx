**Dear passengers!**

According to Article 33 of the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated April 2, 2010 No. 261-IV “On enforcement proceedings and the status of judicial producers”, if the debtor fails to fulfill the requirements contained in the enforcement document within the prescribed period without valid reasons, the bailiff has the right, and at the request of the recoverer is obliged to issue a resolution on temporary restriction on the debtor’s departure from the Republic Kazakhstan.

In this regard, in order to avoid misunderstandings and claims against the Border Service staff of the National Security Committee of the Republic of Kazakhstan, we kindly ask you to contact the official website of the Ministry of Justice of the Republic of Kazakhstan before leaving for the redistribution of the Republic of Kazakhstan www.adilet.gov.kz to clarify information about your presence in the list of debtors temporarily restricted from leaving the Republic of Kazakhstan.

The Border Service of the National Security Committee of the Republic of Kazakhstan does not have information on the lists of restrictions.

Sincerely, the Border Service of the National Security Service of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

**Rules of visa control when crossing the border** **of the Republic of Kazakhstan**

The rules of visa control when crossing the Kazakh border depend on agreements between the Government of the Republic of Kazakhstan and other states.

These agreements are periodically changed. The most complete and reliable information can be obtained at the consular offices of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Kazakhstan, embassies of the Republic of Kazakhstan in your country.

Official website of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Kazakhstan – www.mfa.kz .

The basis for the passage of persons, vehicles, goods and goods across the State Border is the availability of valid documents for the right of entry of persons into the Republic of Kazakhstan or exit from the Republic of Kazakhstan, documents for vehicles, goods and cargo.

The legislation of the Republics of Kazakhstan establishes the following types of documents for crossing the border by citizens of the Republic of Kazakhstan:

- passport of a citizen of the Republic of Kazakhstan
- diplomatic passport
- service passport
- identity
- card seaman’s passport
- certificate of an aircraft crew member (employee of a transport organization)
- certificate of service personnel of border transfer trains
- identity card of an employee of the intergovernmental courier service
- birth certificate
- certificate of return to the Republic of Kazakhstan

If one of the parents (adoptive parents, guardians or trustees) declares his disagreement on the departure from the Republic of Kazakhstan of a minor citizen of the Republic of Kazakhstan, the issue of the possibility of his departure from the Republic of Kazakhstan is resolved in court.

In the presence of valid travel documents, minor children have the right to cross the State Border of the Republic of Kazakhstan unaccompanied by adults.

The agreement of the Republic of Kazakhstan with a neighboring state may establish a simplified procedure for passing through the State Border of citizens of the Republic of Kazakhstan and a neighboring state in terms of determining documents for the right to enter the Republic of Kazakhstan and exit from the Republic of Kazakhstan

The passage of persons, vehicles, goods and goods across the State Border includes the implementation of border control: checking the grounds for the passage of persons, vehicles, goods and goods across the State Border in order to detect and detain violators of the rules of crossing the State Border of the Republic of Kazakhstan, transported goods and goods that are prohibited by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan to import into the Republic of Kazakhstan or export from the Republic of Kazakhstan.

**Information on the stay of foreigners and stateless persons in the Republic of Kazakhstan**

Dear guests of the capital!

We inform you that from January 11, 2020, temporary registration for all categories of foreigners staying in the Republic of Kazakhstan is canceled in the Republic of Kazakhstan; In case of a stay of more than 30 days, a temporary residence permit is issued to a foreigner. The period of temporary stay in the Republic of Kazakhstan of an immigrant who arrived in the Republic of Kazakhstan in a manner that does not require a visa may not exceed 30 calendar days from the date of crossing the State Border of the Republic of Kazakhstan, a total of 90 calendar days during each period of 180 calendar days, unless another procedure is established by an agreement of the Republic of Kazakhstan with the relevant party or the Government Of the Republic of Kazakhstan. Migration cards for foreign citizens and stateless persons have been canceled since January 11, 2020.

For registration of foreign citizens and stateless persons in the territory of the Republic of Kazakhstan, please contact the official website of the Department of Internal Affairs in Nur-Sultan Ministry of Internal Affairs of the Republic of Kazakhstan – www.dvdastana.kz .
