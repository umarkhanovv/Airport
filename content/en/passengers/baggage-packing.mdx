Luggage packing service is provided at the airport. This service will protect your luggage from damage or contamination. The luggage is wrapped with a film, which is quite light and at the same time durable. An employee of the company will be waiting for you on the spot, who will pack your luggage, without any effort on your part.

**Working hours: 24/7**

**Cost: 2000tg**
