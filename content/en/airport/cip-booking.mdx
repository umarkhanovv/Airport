The airport offers CIP lounge service to international passengers. Check-in for
international flights, customs and passport control can all be completed in the
CIP lounge. On departure an airport staff member meets you and takes you to the
aircraft separately.

Charges, VAT included:

- departing adult — 16,500 tenge
- arriving adult — 11,000 tenge
- children aged 2 to 12 — 50% of the fare
- those seeing passengers off or meeting them — 2,200 tenge

To book, call +7 708 715 08 52
