The terminal of Turkestan International Airport offers a wide range of public catering facilities where passengers can enjoy coffee and other beverages, have light snacks, or dine for lunch and dinner. On the first floor of the terminal, to the left of the entrance, there is the Air Coffee café. In the common area on the second floor, the Café Latte coffee shop is located. To enhance passenger comfort, modern vending machines with a wide selection of drinks and snacks have been installed throughout the airport. This allows passengers to quickly and conveniently purchase necessary items at any time, making their stay at the airport even more comfortable.
