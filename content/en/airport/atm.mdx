Halykbank and Bereke ATMs are located on the ground floor of the passenger terminal. There are also payment terminals installed here, through which you can quickly and safely pay for mobile communications, Internet, utilities, credit, pay taxes and other mandatory payments.

**Working hours: 24/7**
