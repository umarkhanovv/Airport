There is a Duty Free shop in the departure and arrival hall of international flights. Here, upon presentation of a boarding pass, passengers, with the exception of those departing to the countries of the Customs Union, can purchase goods of well–known world brands – perfumes and cosmetics, alcoholic and tobacco products, souvenirs, watches, jewelry and much more.

In addition, the updated department of Kazakh products presents national souvenirs, domestic alcohol and confectionery.
