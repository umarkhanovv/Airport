“Turkistan International Airport” LLP is pleased to offer you VIP / CIP lounge services. VIP lounge is offered to serve commercial, protected and officials of the Republic of Kazakhstan who depart and arrive on domestic flights. The VIP lounge is located to the left of the main entrance to the passenger terminal. Service for 1 departing adult VIP 18,000 tenge with VAT. Service for 1 arriving adult VIP 14,000 tenge with VAT. Service for children from 2 to 12 years old 50% of the established rate. For meeting and seeing off VIP 2000 tenge with VAT.

The airport also offers its passengers a CIP lounge service for international passengers. In the CIP lounge, passengers can check in for international flights, customs and passport control. Upon departure, you will be met by an airport employee on a separate transport. Service for 1 departing adult CIP 15 000 tenge with VAT. Service for 1 arriving adult CIP 10 000 tenge with VAT. Service for children from 2 to 12 years old 50% of the established rate.
