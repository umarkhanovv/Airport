### To connect to the free Internet, you need to perform these actions:

Connect to TURKISTAN CITY FREE WIFI

In the developed window, you need to confirm the terms of the user agreement (check the box)

Specify your phone number to which you will receive a short code in the form of a message

Enter the code

Welcome to the internet!
