**MYD Production** is the exclusive advertising operator at airports and railway
stations.

Our mission: to modernise the image of these places and introduce innovation and
current technology, drawing on how the world's largest airports approach
advertising.

MYD Production means:

- applying international practice;
- personal accountability for advertising content;
- one architectural style, integrated into the site;
- clear payment discipline;
- our own laboratory for designing and building advertising structures;
- working with international partners in advertising.
